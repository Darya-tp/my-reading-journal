# 📚 My Reading Journal

*A little home for every story I've ever loved.*

My Reading Journal is a personal, customizable digital space for book lovers who want to do more than simply track what they've read.

It's a place to collect memories, favorite quotes, beautiful artwork, and all the feelings a good book leaves behind.

### ✨ Features

- 📖 Personal library with covers, ratings, and reading dates
- 🎨 Custom page themes, fonts, colors, and layouts
- 🖼️ Fan art, moodboards, and movable stickers
- ✍️ Reviews, summaries, tropes, and text highlighting
- 💬 Quote Vault for favorite passages
- 👑 Hall of Favorites
- 📚 Series tracking and reading statistics
- 💾 Backup and restore
- 📱 Responsive layouts for mobile and desktop
- 🌙 Offline-friendly Progressive Web App (PWA)

### 💙 Made Personal

Every book deserves its own little corner of the universe. Customize your pages, decorate your memories, and make your reading journal truly yours.

### 🔗 Try It

[Open My Reading Journal](https://darya-tp.github.io/my-reading-journal/)

### 🔒 Privacy

Reading data is stored locally in your browser. No account is required, and data does not automatically sync between devices.
