My Reading Journal v0.9

NEW: Marker brush mode. Tap the floating crayon in Edit view, pick one of 8 colours, then drag your S Pen or finger across the text in Summary, Tropes, My Review or Favourite Quotes. While you drag, the text is temporarily previewed in the marker colour; on release, the colour becomes a real text highlight. Tap one word to highlight it. Tap the eraser and drag to remove highlights. Tap the crayon again to return to normal typing and text selection.

Previous selection-to-highlight remains available when the marker is off. Highlights appear in locked Reading View and are included in backups.

The standalone HTML runs without installing anything. For PWA installation, upload this entire folder to an HTTPS host. Open index.html or the standalone HTML to test it. Real S Pen device behaviour needs user testing.

IMPORTANT: Export a backup in your current journal, then use Import in this new version. Local files and browser origins might have separate storage.
